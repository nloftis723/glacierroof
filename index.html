What it does not do yet, on purpose

No database, no accounts, no PDF, no attachments, no audit trail, no MCP. This runs from a folder so the estimators can argue with the rules before anything is built on Azure. Quotes live in the browser tab until exported to a file.
Parity testing is the next gate: load 10 to 15 historical quotes, enter the same takeoff here, and reconcile to the dollar. Use the workbook pricing and workbook rules settings for that run, since history was priced with the defects in place.
